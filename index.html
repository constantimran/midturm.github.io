🛡️ Safe Buying Tips
Avoid Too-Good-to-Be-True Deals

Discounts over 40% on major brands are almost always scams. If it seems unreal, trust your instincts and move on.

Always Use Buyer Protection

Never pay via wire transfer or crypto outside the platform. Use only platform-protected checkout so disputes can be resolved.

Prioritize Verified Sellers

Look for sellers with 4.5+ stars, 100+ completed trades, and recent positive reviews. New sellers with zero history carry more risk.

Test Codes Immediately

Redeem your gift card code as soon as you receive it, while buyer protection is still active. Don't wait — codes can be invalidated.

Report Suspicious Activity

If a seller asks you to move off-platform or share personal info outside the app, report them immediately and don't complete the trade.

Keep Records

Screenshot your purchase confirmation and the card code before redeeming. This helps resolve disputes quickly and protects your purchase.
